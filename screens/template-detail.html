Invoice approval with Slack sign-off

Finance · by AutoFlow · v4 · 1,204 installs · updated 23 Jul 2026

What it does

An invoice arrives on a webhook. A model pulls out the vendor, amount, currency, and due date. Anything above your threshold pauses for a human in Slack; anything below it is approved automatically. Either way the outcome is written back to your ledger, and the run trace shows which branch was taken and why.

Typical run: 3.9 seconds, about 2,100 tokens, $0.003. The approval branch can hold a run open for up to 24 hours.
