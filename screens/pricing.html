Questions we actually get

Does a failed run count?

Yes. It consumed compute and produced a trace. A run that never starts — one rejected at the webhook — does not.

What happens when I hit the limit?

On Pro, overage bills at $1 per 1,000 runs. On Free, new runs queue and you get an email. Nothing is silently dropped.

Can I use my own model keys?

On every plan, including Free. Your keys are stored envelope-encrypted and cannot be read back — not by you, not by us.

Can I self-host?

Not yet. It’s on the Phase 3 roadmap and we’d rather say so than sell you a date.
